## Brand & Style

The design system establishes an executive-grade, performance-driven aesthetic that merges precision horology with high-performance biometric technology. It bridges the corporate boardroom and elite athletic environments, projecting authority, technological supremacy, and calculated refinement.

### Target Audience
Executive professionals, endurance athletes, and tech-forward high performers who demand seamless transitions between formal business environments and rigorous physical training. They prioritize durability, precision telemetry, and minimalist luxury.

### Emotional Signature
- **Precision & Mastery:** Evoked through sharp micro-details, technical line weights, and engineered typographic rhythms.
- **Uncompromised Luxury:** Deep onyx surfaces, subtle metallic graphite reflections, and controlled luminous accents.
- **High-Stakes Focus:** Decisive visual hierarchy, glare-free dark surfaces, and immediate data readability.

### Style Archetype: Precision Dark Glass & Metallic Depth
A bespoke fusion of **Dark Tech Minimalist** and **Refined Glassmorphism**. The interface avoids flat, generic dark mode patterns in favor of multi-layered obsidian depth, hairline borders with metallic micro-gradients, and calculated specular highlights mimicking brushed titanium and sapphire crystal. Electric cyan and emerald green are deployed strictly as focal beacons for telemetry and conversion, preserving visual exclusivity.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around an uncompromising pitch-black foundation layered with metallic graphite and illuminated by precise photonic emitters.

### Palette Hierarchy & Intent
- **Primary (`#00E5FF` - Electric Cyan):** High-energy photonic highlight. Used exclusively for primary calls to action, active tracking state indicators, and conversion anchors.
- **Secondary (`#0070F3` - Deep Kinetic Blue):** Anchors linear gradients, structural hover states, and premium badge outlines. Delivers depth when paired with Electric Cyan.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981` - Emerald Biometric):** Dedicated telemetry color. Denotes vitality, cardiovascular stability, recovery score, battery health, and verified authenticity markers.
- **Neutral Base (`#0B0D11` - Onyx Core):** Deep, pure-void neutral with an ultra-subtle cold metallic undertone. Eliminates visual fatigue and enhances specular contrast.

### Surface Tones & Metal Finishes
- `surface-0` (`#07080A`): Canvas background and global baseline.
- `surface-1` (`#0F1218`): Core card and container level with deep obsidian tone.
- `surface-2` (`#161B24`): Elevated glass layers, active tooltips, floating navigational bars.
- `border-hairline` (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`): Technical separation line.
- `border-accent` (`rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.25)`): Selected states and hover triggers.
- `text-primary` (`#F8FAFC`): Cold pure white for primary titles and critical metrics.
- `text-secondary` (`#94A3B8`): Muted graphite-silver for narrative body copy.
- `text-tertiary` (`#64748B`): Technical captions, serial timestamps, and specifications.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy orchestrates a high-tech instrument cluster aesthetic. 

- **Display & Headlines (Sora):** Provides geometric precision, architectural authority, and contemporary technical elegance. Large display titles utilize negative letter tracking to create density and forward velocity.
- **Narrative Copy (Hanken Grotesk):** Delivers clean readability with humanist clarity, counteracting the coldness of raw technical layouts.
- **Telemetry & Metadata (JetBrains Mono):** Injects watchmaker calibration marks, sensor readouts, battery metrics, and authenticity certifications. Rendered in uppercase with generous tracking to reinforce industrial engineering.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is governed by a technical 12-column grid on desktop, scaling to 6 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile. Maximum layout canvas is constrained to 1280px to ensure dense, scan-friendly visual units resembling luxury instrument consoles.

### Structural Flow & Rhythms
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column fluid structure with 80px (`margin-desktop`) canvas padding and 24px (`gutter`) inter-column channels. Sections are separated by generous optical breathers (`space-3xl`) to spotlight product industrial renders.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 6-column grid with 32px margins and 20px gutters. Dual-card executive vs. athlete matrices collapse into a stacked 1:1 format.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column compact architecture with 20px (`margin-mobile`) side gutters. Product specs and telemetry dials shift into horizontally scrollable or accordion-grouped cards.

### Dense Scan Units
Micro-content within feature blocks follows an 8px base rhythm. Element clusters (e.g., metric value + unit + label) utilize tight internal gaps (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) to preserve data cohesion, while separating functional modules with `space-lg`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through structural layering of darkened crystalline plates rather than dramatic ambient light sources.

### Depth Architecture
1. **Base Layer (Surface 0):** Background void `#07080A` embedded with a faint radial gradient emitting from behind the 3D watch assets (`rgba(0, 112, 243, 0.08)` blur 120px).
2. **Glassmorphic Substrates (Surface 1):** Semi-transparent `#0F1218` at 70% opacity backed by `backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(180%)`. Bordered by a razor-thin 1px top-to-bottom metallic gradient stroke: `linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.14) 0%, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02) 100%)`.
3. **Floating Command Consoles (Surface 2 & Sticky CTA):** `#161B24` at 85% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`. Augmented by an ambient tinted floor shadow: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7), 0 0 20px -5px rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.15)`.
4. **Specular Micro-Bevels:** Cards feature an inner box-shadow rim `inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` simulating diamond-cut chamfered edges of luxury watch casings.

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, controlled roundedness (`0.5rem` / `8px` default radius, scaling to `1rem` / `16px` for containers) to echo the precise curve of a forged titanium watch chassis with curved sapphire glass.

### Radii Hierarchy
- **Micro Radii (`0.25rem` / `4px`):** Status indicators, technical badges, sensor pills, and telemetry sparkline markers.
- **Control Radii (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Primary buttons, interactive tabs, form inputs, and comparison toggles.
- **Structural Radii (`1rem` / `16px`):** Feature glass cards, performance metric displays, and interactive showcase modals.
- **Hero Surface Radii (`1.5rem` / `24px`):** The primary floating sticky CTA bar and persistent viewport dock.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Sticky / Conversion Button:** 
  - Fill: Linear directional gradient from Electric Cyan to Deep Kinetic Blue (`linear-gradient(135deg, #00E5FF 0%, #0070F3 100%)`).
  - Text: Contrast dark `#07080A`, weight 600 (`label-btn`), uppercase tracking.
  - Edge: 1px inner bright glow `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`.
  - Hover: Subtle luminance amplification with cyan atmospheric halo `box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.4)`.
- **Secondary Executive Button:**
  - Fill: Obsidian glass `rgba(22, 27, 36, 0.6)`.
  - Border: 1px hairline `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
  - Text: `#F8FAFC`.
  - Hover: Border switches to `rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.4)`, text shifts to `#00E5FF`.

### Sticky CTA Bar
- Position: Persistent floating dock pinned to the bottom viewport (`bottom: 1.5rem`), centered with `max-width: 720px` on desktop and full width (`margin-mobile`) on mobile.
- Surface: Deep graphite glass `#0F1218E6`, `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`, chamfered top hairline border.
- Layout: Dual cluster displaying current pre-order batch, live allocation counter, discounted executive tier price, and high-impact action button.

### High-Scan Telemetry & Feature Cards
- Construction: Surface-1 obsidian glass containers with diamond-cut chamfer borders.
- Header: Monospaced metadata tags in top corners (e.g., `SYS.SENS_01`, `RECOVERY_RATE`).
- Visual Focal Point: Real-time kinetic metric dial, battery curve, or titanium alloy macro-shot.
- Metric Display: High-contrast Sora numeral flanked by Emerald Green (`#10B981`) delta tags (e.g., `+14.2% HRV OPTIMAL`).

### Authenticity & Specification Badges
- Surface: `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.08)` fill for health/durability certifications; `rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.08)` for technical speed indicators.
- Border: 1px hairline border matching the corresponding accent color.
- Typography: `label-badge` (JetBrains Mono, uppercase, 0.12em tracking).
- Iconography: Integrated 12px micro-icon (e.g., Sapphire Shield, 100M Water Resistance seal, Titanium Grade 5 certification mark).

### Interactive Executive-Sport Dual Switcher
- Mechanism: Segmented glass toggle allowing seamless interface refactor between "Boardroom Chrono" (onyx, dual time-zone, calendar alerts) and "Athletic Telemetry" (VO2 max, heart rate zones, GPS tracking).
- Active Pill: Dark metallic surface with Electric Cyan edge glow and smooth 200ms cubic-bezier transition.
